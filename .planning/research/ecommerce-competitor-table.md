## Key Insights

1. **Shopify dominates** Romanian e-commerce — only Netscroll uses WooCommerce
2. **COD (Cash on Delivery)** is still critical in Romania — most stores support it
3. **SMS/WhatsApp OTP** for order confirmation is a competitive advantage
4. **Judge.me** is the review platform of choice for Shopify stores
5. **Teleshopero leads** in sophistication: A/B testing, 180+ active ads, 2-3M EUR/year
6. **Relaxu** has strongest social proof: 140K+ reviews on Judge.me
